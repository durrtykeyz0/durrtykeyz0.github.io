Straight answers

Does it need the internet? +

No. That's the entire point. The guides open as plain documents and the encyclopedia packs run in the free Kiwix reader — everything works with zero connection. Put your phone in airplane mode and test it yourself.

What devices does it work on? +

Windows, Mac, Android, and iOS. The USB drive has both USB-C and USB-A connectors and is formatted exFAT so phones and computers read it without fuss. The encyclopedia packs use Kiwix, which is free on all four platforms.

What's the difference between the book and the drive? +

The drive (or download) is the full library: all the guides, all the checklists, plus ~17.8 GB of offline reference — Wikipedia, WikiMed, Ready.gov, and the manuals. The book, OFFLINE ARK: The Grid-Down Survival Handbook, is the core guides in print — the version that works when every battery you own is dead. They pair well; neither requires the other.
